## Hamas's Attacks of October 2023

The UN Human Rights Council report 
https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/sessions-regular/session56/a-hrc-56-crp-3.pdf

There On the 7th of October 2023, armed Hamas militants, coordinated an attack breaching Gaza's prison wall in ten different locations along its perimeter and attacking the surrounding military bases and kibbutz (Israeli villages). Hundreds of Gaza civilians also broke out and took food, horses and other goods from surrounding small urban areas back into Gaza. As they moved outwards through the territory, Hamas militia killed first military personnel and armed security forces, often, then unarmed civilians on roads, a music festival and kibbutz, taking hostages back to Gaza. They were not expecting so little resistance from the Israeli security forces. Video footage they themselves took shows that when they arrived to the kibbutz they were aimless in their disorganized assault on the inhabitants. By then, Israeli military and security forces deployed helicopters and tanks. Video footage from these war machines shows the indiscriminate use of heavy weaponry on cars going back into Gaza as well as kibbutz homes, likely an implementation of the Hannibal directives (don't leave hostages). The casualty of Israeli and foreign nationals was 1,150. Of these approximately 250 were soldiers, 50 police, 60 civilian security personnel, and 800 unarmed civilians, of which 36 children (13 under the age of 12, 2 of which babies).

This is horrible. Hamas militants committed war crimes that day. 

The Israeli news media largely focused their reporting on allegations made by military, security personnel and [ZAKA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZAKA) voluteers that have not been substantiated by any evidence, in particular the systematic beheading and killing of babies, children put killed in ovens, children tied to the back and burnt to death, torture and amputations, systematic rape of women. These lies were amplified by western media, were repeated by Netaniahu, the US president Biden and members of its administration (such as the secretary of state Blinken) with the purpose to maximize the horror in the public opinion and manufacture consent for the "Israel has the right to defend itself" narrative, including the perpetuation of the war crimes and the genocide that followed. 

Israel's corrected the casualty figures (originally ~ 1400) later saying that 200 bodies were palestinian, but because were so severely burnt or mutilated it was difficult to identify them. Israel blocked in independent investigation on the systematic rape allegations. 

Hamas trained for the incursion and posted videos online. Israel was in possession of the details of the invasion plan, which reached the chief of the military intelligence.

How could I possibly contribute to the already mountainous body of video, images, and text that documents, analyzes, and prescribes solutions to the horrors inflicted on the native people of Palestine by Israel and its allies since the disgraceful decision to manufacture the State of Israel in 1948?

What else could I add to the synthesis work of scholars like[Edward Said](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Said), [Noam Chomsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky), [Ilan Pappé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilan_Papp%C3%A9), [Norman Finkelstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Finkelstein), and [Rashid Khalidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashid_Khalidi), to the reporting work of so many journalists and individuals who have sacrificed so much to uphold the truth?

Then I spoke to my parents, who reminded me of the importance of one's close social group, which generally inspires superior trust and empathy, which can serve as an enzyme to catalyze the process of overcoming powerful energetic barriers to reaching truth and acting on it:

- The Matrix: the systemic deception to manufacture your consent for your disempowerment and exploitation.
- Analytical laziness: the middle ground fallacy (*"The truth lies in the middle"*), is among the worst forms of dogmatism, the antithesis of intellectual inquiry. One needs to put in real work to uncover and consider the evidence, distinguish facts from deception.
- Denial: an understandable defense mechanism against the immorality, cruelty and horror of reality.
- Conformism: the tendency to avoid social friction and fit in the average of your social network.
